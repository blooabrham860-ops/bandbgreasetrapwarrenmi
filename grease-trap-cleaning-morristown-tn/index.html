Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should a commercial grease trap be cleaned?

Most municipalities require grease traps to be cleaned once the combined volume of fats, oils, and grease and solids reaches 25% of the total liquid depth of the trap. For many busy restaurants, this translates to grease interceptor cleaning near me every 1 to 3 months, though high-volume kitchens may need monthly service.

2. What happens if you don't clean a grease trap?

Failing to perform grease interceptor maintenance leads to the FOG hardening, which blocks the outlet pipe and causes sewage to back up into the kitchen sinks or floor drains. Additionally, the decomposition of accumulated food waste releases sulfuric acid, which damages the steel or concrete tank and creates foul, rotten-egg odors.

3. Can I use enzymes instead of pumping my grease trap?

While biological additives and enzymes can help break down some fats, they are not a replacement for physical pumping. They can sometimes emulsify grease, allowing it to pass down the line and re-solidify later, causing municipal blockages. The best way to clean grease trap infrastructure is always professional evacuation and scraping.

4. Who is responsible for grease trap maintenance?

In almost all jurisdictions, the business owner (the waste generator) is legally responsible for the maintenance and condition of the grease trap. Even if you lease the building, the fines for non-compliance or city sewer blockages caused by your kitchen fall on your business, making reliable trap maintenance essential.

5. Why does my grease trap smell after cleaning?

If a smell persists after cleaning, it often means the gasket on the trap lid is worn out or not seated correctly, allowing sewer gas to escape. It could also indicate that the vent line is clogged or that the service provider did not scrape the bacterial buildup from the walls of the trap.

Morristown, TN Insights: Population,
Zip Codes, Influence, and Service Areas

Morristown is a city in and the county seat of Hamblen County, Tennessee, United States. Morristown also extends into Jefferson County on the western and southern ends. It is the principal city of the Morristown Metropolitan Statistical Area, which encompasses all of Grainger, Hamblen, and Jefferson counties.
